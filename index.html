How can we improve our visibility for sizing questions?

✳   Orymus

Start with your jeans size guide. In this sample, 160STORE appeared in 10% of answers about fit and sizing.

Here are three things to try:

  1. Update the jeans size guide. Add waist and inseam measurements by size.
  2. Explain each fit. Describe how slim, regular and relaxed jeans fit, and link to the guide from product pages.
  3. Run the same sizing prompts again. Check whether more answers mention 160STORE or link to its size guide.
